How a toy works

Your browser talks to a tiny Worker

You sign in with AIVAX and approve a key limited to what the toys need. A Cloudflare Worker keeps it in a cookie the page can't read.

The Worker calls AIVAX

Each call is a plain HTTPS request to the public AIVAX API, billed to your account. The Worker forwards the result, plus timing, request ID and usage.

You see the real numbers

The session panel lists every call, its latency and processing units, and keeps a running cost estimate. The exact charges are in your AIVAX console.
